# Author notes for the introduction

- Main message: test loss of Transformer LMs follows power laws in N, D and C; compute-efficient training uses large models stopped early.
- Related lines: (1) empirical scaling studies of generalization error vs data/model size (Hestness et al. 2017; Rosenfeld et al. 2019); (2) large Transformer LMs (Vaswani et al. 2017; Radford et al. 2019).
- Vaswani et al. (2017) introduced the Transformer architecture used by all our models.
